## 2. The ARR reviewer: what is different

ACL 2027 is reviewed through ACL Rolling Review (January 2027 cycle). Reviewers fill the ARR
form (aclrollingreview.org/reviewform); an area chair writes a meta-review, and the
paper is then committed to the conference. The review-panel plugin's ARR prompt is
`review-panel/prompts/naive-arrform.md`.

| Field | Scale | What moves it |
|---|---|---|
| Overall Assessment | 1-5 in half steps: 5 award, 4 conference, 3.5 borderline conference, 3 Findings, 2.5 borderline Findings, 2 resubmit next cycle, 1.5 resubmit after next cycle, 1 do not resubmit | 4 vs 3 is main conference vs Findings; most arguments move this one step |
| Soundness | 1-5 in half steps: 5 excellent, 4 strong, 3 acceptable, 2 poor, 1 major issues | every claim backed by an experiment; baselines and ablations; honest limits |
| Excitement | 1-5 in half steps: 5 highly exciting ... 1 not exciting | subjective; a clear statement of why the result matters to the *ACL community |
| Reviewer Confidence | 1-5: 5 positive ... 1 not my area | a low-confidence negative review is easier to answer in the author response |
| Reproducibility | 1-5: 5 could easily reproduce ... 1 could not | code, data, hyperparameters, seeds, compute stated |
| Datasets, Software | 1-5: 5 enabling ... 1 none released | released artifacts, documented |

The form also asks about Limitations and Societal Impact, Ethical Concerns, and the
reviewer's guess at author identity. The Limitations section is required (desk reject
without it) and must not add new results.

Write for a reviewer who reads the abstract, the figures, and the table captions
first, then decides how much of the body to read.
