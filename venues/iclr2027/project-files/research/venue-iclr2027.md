## 2. The ICLR reviewer: what is different

Reviewers fill one form per paper. The scales below are the ones the review-panel
plugin drives; the full prompt text lives in `review-panel/prompts/iclr-form.md`,
read it before you write for this form rather than copying it here.

| Field | Scale | What moves it |
|---|---|---|
| rating (overall) | even values only: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 | 6 is the acceptance threshold; 4 and 6 are the two scores most arguments actually shift |
| soundness | 1-4 | claims matched by evidence; ablations; honest limits |
| presentation | 1-4 | a reader can find the claim, the setup, and the result without re-reading |
| contribution | 1-4 | what is new against the closest prior work, stated in the paper |
| confidence | 1-5 | how sure the reviewer is; a low-confidence reject is easier to move than a confident one |

Write for a reviewer who reads the abstract, the figures, and the table captions
first, then decides how much of the body to read.
